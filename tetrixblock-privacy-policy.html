Tetrix Block Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tetrix Block mobile application handles user information. Tetrix Block is developed by OGUZ STUDIO.

1. Information We Collect

Tetrix Block is designed to keep game-related data primarily on the user's device. Game data such as the player name, scores, statistics, settings, progress, and similar gameplay information may be stored locally on the device.

The application is not designed to collect directly identifying personal information such as a user's name, address, or phone number unless the user voluntarily provides such information through a relevant service.

2. Advertising

The free version of Tetrix Block may display advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob may process device and usage-related information for purposes such as serving, measuring, and personalizing advertisements, in accordance with Google's policies and applicable law.

For more information about how Google handles data, please see the Google Privacy Policy .

3. Google Play and Premium Purchases

Tetrix Block uses Google Play Billing for Premium in-app purchases. Payment information is processed directly by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store users' credit card or bank card information.

Premium purchase status may be verified through Google Play. Premium features are enabled according to the user's purchase status.

4. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

The core gameplay and locally stored game data are designed to work without an internet connection. An internet connection may be required for services such as loading advertisements and processing Google Play purchases.

Tetrix Block does not require users to create an account or use mandatory cloud storage for its core game data.

5. Data Sharing

OGUZ STUDIO does not sell or rent users' personal information. However, third-party services used by the application, such as Google AdMob and Google Play, may process information necessary to provide their services in accordance with their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Tetrix Block is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or legal guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information through the application may contact the developer through the contact channels available on Google Play.

7. Data Security

The security of locally stored game data depends in part on the security of the user's device and operating system. Users should use appropriate device security measures to protect their data.

8. Deleting Data

Users can delete locally stored game data by using the data reset or clear-data options available within the application. Android's application settings may also provide an option to clear the application's stored data.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page and will become effective when posted.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Tetrix Block's data practices, you can contact the developer through the developer contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
