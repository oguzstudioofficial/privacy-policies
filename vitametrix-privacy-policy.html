VitaMetrix – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

VitaMetrix: Health & Fitness is a health and fitness application developed by OGUZ STUDIO. This Privacy Policy explains how VitaMetrix handles information when you use the app.

1. No account or login is required

VitaMetrix does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, username, telephone number, password, or other login credentials.

2. Information you enter

VitaMetrix allows you to enter health and fitness information such as body measurements, weight, body-fat information, muscle and water measurements, nutrition records, water intake, sleep records, exercise and workout records, supplements, personal records, goals, and related app settings.

3. How your information is stored

The app is designed for local/offline use. The information you enter is stored locally on your device for the app's features. VitaMetrix does not operate a user account system or require you to upload your health and fitness records to an OGUZ STUDIO server.

4. Sharing of information

OGUZ STUDIO does not sell your personal information or your health and fitness records. VitaMetrix does not intentionally transmit your entered health and fitness records to OGUZ STUDIO for advertising or profiling.

5. Data export

If you use the app's data export feature, the exported data is provided to you so that you can save, transfer, or otherwise use your own data. You are responsible for how an exported file is stored or shared after export.

6. Google Play Billing and Premium features

VitaMetrix offers optional Premium features through Google Play Billing. Purchases and payment processing are handled by Google Play. OGUZ STUDIO does not receive or store your full payment card details through VitaMetrix. The app may receive purchase and entitlement information from Google Play so that Premium access can be granted or restored.

7. Advertising and analytics

VitaMetrix does not intentionally collect personal information for behavioral advertising or user profiling. The app does not require an advertising account or user account to function.

8. Children's privacy

VitaMetrix is a general health and fitness application and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not require an account, it does not ask users to submit identifying information to create an account.

9. Security

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. You should protect access to your device and avoid sharing exported files containing personal health or fitness information with people you do not trust.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions about VitaMetrix, contact OGUZ STUDIO through the developer contact information provided on its Google Play listing.
